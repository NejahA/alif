# Comprehensive List of Known Alphabets and Writing Systems
# Compiled by Grok - June 2026

## 1. Proto-Writing and Early Systems (Before 3000 BCE)
- Nsibidi (Nigeria, Cameroon) - Ideographic system
- Adinkra symbols (Akan people, Ghana)
- Vinča symbols (Europe, ~5500 BCE) - disputed
- Jiahu symbols (China, ~6600 BCE)
- Tartaria tablets (Romania)

## 2. Ancient Logographic Systems
- Egyptian Hieroglyphs (~3200 BCE)
- Sumerian Cuneiform (~3400 BCE)
- Chinese Oracle Bone Script (~1200 BCE) → Modern Chinese characters
- Mayan Hieroglyphs (Mesoamerica)
- Hittite / Anatolian Hieroglyphs
- Dongba (Naxi people, China)

## 3. Syllabaries
- Linear B (Mycenaean Greek, ~1400 BCE)
- Cypriot syllabary
- Japanese Kana:
  - Hiragana
  - Katakana
- Cherokee syllabary (Sequoyah, 1821)
- Vai syllabary (Liberia)
- Yi syllabary (China)

## 4. Abjads (Consonant Alphabets)
- Phoenician ( ~1050 BCE) - Ancestor of most Western alphabets
- Aramaic
- Hebrew
- Arabic
- Nabataean
- Syriac
- Ugaritic cuneiform alphabet (~1300 BCE)
- Ancient South Arabian
- Ancient North Arabian

## 5. True Alphabets (Consonant + Vowel)
- Greek (~800 BCE)
- Latin (Roman) - Most used today
- Etruscan
- Runic (Elder Futhark, ~150 CE)
- Ogham (Ireland, ~4th century)
- Gothic (Ulfilas, 4th century)
- Armenian (Mesrop Mashtots, 405 CE)
- Georgian (Asomtavruli, Nuskhuri, Mkhedruli)
- Coptic
- Old Hungarian (Szekely)
- Glagolitic (9th century)

## 6. Cyrillic and Related
- Cyrillic (9th-10th century)
- Glagolitic (predecessor)
- Modern variants: Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Serbian, etc.

## 7. Abugidas (Aksara / Alphasyllabaries)
- Brahmi script (India, ~3rd century BCE) - Ancestor of many
  - Devanagari (Hindi, Sanskrit, Marathi, Nepali)
  - Bengali-Assamese
  - Tamil
  - Telugu
  - Kannada
  - Malayalam
  - Sinhala
- Thai
- Lao
- Khmer
- Tibetan
- Ethiopic / Ge'ez (Ethiopia, Eritrea)
- Javanese
- Balinese

## 8. Featural Alphabets
- Hangul (Korean, 1443 CE) - Most scientifically designed
- Canadian Aboriginal Syllabics

## 9. Other / Modern
- Braille
- International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)
- Deseret Alphabet (Mormon, 19th century)
- Shavian Alphabet
- Manual alphabets (Sign languages)
- Blissymbols

## Historical Timeline Summary
- ~3400 BCE: Cuneiform (earliest)
- ~3200 BCE: Egyptian Hieroglyphs
- ~1800 BCE: Proto-Sinaitic (first alphabet-like)
- ~1050 BCE: Phoenician
- ~800 BCE: Greek (first true alphabet with vowels)
- 405 CE: Armenian
- 1443: Hangul
- 19th-21st century: Many indigenous scripts created

Note: This is a high-level overview. There are over 100 distinct writing systems historically. Many scripts have evolved into multiple variants.
