Why Truetooth?

Everything you need to take control of your audio experience.

Active Noise Cancellation

Three modes: Off, ANC, and Transparency. Switch seamlessly between total immersion and awareness.

🎛️

8-Band Equalizer

Choose from 8 presets — Flat, Bass Boost, Treble Boost, Vocal, Classical, Rock, Jazz, or create your own.

Spatial Audio

Head-tracked spatial audio that stays locked to your device, not your head. Immersive soundstage.
